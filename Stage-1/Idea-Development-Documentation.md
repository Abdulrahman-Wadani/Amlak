### 1. Ideas Considered

**Idea 1: Fitness Coaching Platform (Rejected)**

* **Concept:** An application designed to connect gym trainers with trainees, offering tools and services to help both parties achieve their fitness and training goals efficiently.
* **Strengths:** Addresses a growing health and fitness market; promotes physical well-being; provides a direct B2C (Business-to-Consumer) engagement model with high user retention potential.
* **Weaknesses:** The fitness app market is highly saturated with strong local and global competitors. It also requires complex credential verification for trainers and relies on building a massive user base to become profitable.
* **Reason for Rejection:** The team decided against this idea because the barrier to entry is high, and the competitive landscape is too crowded. We wanted to focus on a B2B (Business-to-Business) model where a specific, unaddressed operational pain point could be solved for a niche market willing to pay for immediate value.

### 2. Summary of the Selected MVP: "Amlak"

* **Concept:** A comprehensive property management system specifically tailored for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) managing chalets and sports fields. The system allows owners to input their properties, track booked days over the month, manage maintenance schedules and costs, and generate complete financial and operational analytics.
* **Business Model:** A B2B SaaS (Software as a Service) model generating revenue through monthly subscriptions paid by property owners. Subscriptions range from 300 SAR to 1000 SAR, tiered based on the number of properties and unlocked features. Future scalability includes annual plans, premium add-ons, and commission fees on direct bookings and electronic payments.
* **Rationale for Selection:** "Amlak" addresses a critical gap in the local market. Many chalet and sports field owners currently lack centralized digital systems, relying instead on manual tracking or fragmented tools. By offering a dedicated B2B solution, "Amlak" guarantees a clearer, more predictable revenue stream compared to the fitness app, targeting businesses that will see an immediate return on investment.
* **Potential Impact:** The MVP will digitally transform how local SMEs manage their real estate assets. It will eliminate manual errors, prevent double-bookings, optimize maintenance scheduling to prevent costly damages, and provide owners with clear financial visibility (revenues vs. expenses) to maximize their overall profitability.

### 3. Team Formation and Process Followed

* **Team Formation:** The team was assembled based on complementary technical and business skills necessary to execute a B2B SaaS product. Roles were distributed to cover software development, business analysis, product design, and market research, ensuring all aspects of the system's lifecycle—from coding the analytics dashboard to structuring the subscription tiers—were handled by capable members.
* **Process Followed:**
1. **Brainstorming:** The team conducted initial ideation sessions, generating multiple concepts across different industries (including fitness and real estate).
2. **Market & Problem Analysis:** We evaluated the generated ideas against market needs. We identified the severe lack of digital management tools for local chalet and sports field owners.
3. **Feasibility & Selection:** We compared the Fitness Coaching Platform against "Amlak." We analyzed the target audience, monetization potential, and competitive landscape, ultimately voting unanimously for "Amlak" due to its stronger business model and clearer target audience.
4. **MVP Definition:** We outlined the core features required for launch—property listing, maintenance tracking, booking calendars, and basic financial analytics—and structured the pricing tiers (300–1000 SAR) to align with SME budgets.
