# Amlak

Amlak is a management system for small and medium-sized businesses that own or manage assets, such as chalets and rental projects.

The system helps business owners track their **assets, income, expenses, and expected future costs** over periods such as 6 months or 1 year.

## Objectives

* Manage projects and assets in one system.
* Track income and expenses.
* Estimate future costs and financial needs.
* Provide a simple dashboard to understand business performance.

## Stakeholders & Roles

* **Business Owners:** Main users of the system.
* **Project Team:** Design, develop, and test Amlak.
* **Project Supervisor:** Provides guidance and feedback.
* **End Users:** Test the system and provide feedback.

## Scope

### In Scope

* User accounts
* Project and asset management
* Income and expense tracking
* Financial summaries
* Future cost estimation
* Dashboard and basic reports


### Out of Scope

* Banking integration
* Full accounting
* Payroll
* Government system integration

## Risks

| Risk                                          | Mitigation                                         |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Business owners may not recognize the problem | Interview users and demonstrate the value of Amlak |
| Lack of user feedback                         | Test the system with potential users               |
| Limited development time                      | Focus on the core MVP features                     |
| Technical problems                            | Test each component throughout development         |
